Welcome to Degen Decks

Embark on a cosmic adventure in the most legendary space pirate den in the galaxy. Degen Decks is where fearless captains gather to test their luck, skill, and fortune in the depths of an asteroid hideout. Whether you're here to mint your doubloons, join epic raffles, or dominate the poker tables, you've found the space to do it.

DDD Token System

Our Degen Deck Doubloons (DDD) are the lifeblood of this cosmic facility. Wrap your USDC at a fixed 1:1000 rate to get DDD tokens, or unwrap them back when you need liquidity. The vault system ensures the tokencirculation, creates a feeless exchange, secured on the Solana blockchain for maximum transparency.

Cosmic Raffles

Test your cosmic luck in our legendary raffles! Enter with ATLAS or DDD tokens for a chance to win incredible prizes. Each raffle is a unique adventure with different entry costs and prize pools. Watch the cosmic dice roll and see if fortune favors the bold. Multiple entries increase your odds of claiming the ultimate prize!

Poker in the Stars

Join the most intense poker games this side of the asteroid belt! Open-seat tables welcome all skill levels for casual games, while our tournaments offer the ultimate test of strategy and nerve. Compete against fellow galactic players for glory, ATLAS, DDD tokens, bragging rights across the galaxy, new spaceships and more!
